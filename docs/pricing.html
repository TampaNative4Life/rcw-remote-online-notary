Travel Pricing

Distance affects the mobile fee.

Mobile appointments start from the RCW Sr. service area in ZIP Code 33594.

Travel charges are based on round-trip driving distance and the current RCW travel calculation rate.

The RCW travel calculation rate is based on the current local premium gasoline benchmark plus a $1.00 operating allowance.

The final calculated travel charge is rounded up to the next whole dollar.
