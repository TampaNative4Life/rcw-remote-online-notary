The Business

Why RCW Sr. exists.

A lot of people do not live on a nine-to-five schedule. Some work nights. Some are caring for family. Some have transportation problems. Some are recovering from illness or surgery. Some are seniors or have limited mobility. Some simply need something handled after banks and offices are closed.

RCW Sr. Notary Services is designed around those situations.

The business combines Remote Online Notary service with mobile appointments so clients can choose the option that works best for them.

Service Philosophy

Your schedule comes first.

My normal hours are 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM Eastern. That gives clients a large scheduling window without forcing them into traditional banking hours.

Earlier and later appointments may also be considered by request.

For clients outside the Eastern Time Zone, especially West Coast clients, scheduling is handled with your local time in mind.

Important Role of the Notary

A notary verifies. A notary does not give legal advice.

RCW Sr. Notary Services performs eligible notarial acts and follows Florida notary requirements.

A notary cannot choose legal documents for you, tell you which notarial certificate you need, interpret your legal rights, or provide legal advice.
