Privacy Policy

Your information should be handled carefully.

This Privacy Policy explains what information RCW Sr. Notary Services may collect through this website, why it is collected, how it may be used, and how it is handled.

The goal is to collect only the information reasonably needed to answer questions, prepare estimates, schedule appointments, and provide notary services.

Privacy Summary

Keep it limited.

Do not submit Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, passwords, access codes, or full document contents through the general website appointment form unless specifically instructed to use a secure process.

Privacy Policy

Effective August 2026.

01

Who This Policy Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the RCW Sr. Notary Services website and through appointment requests submitted through the website.

Separate privacy, security, identity-verification, recording, and record-retention requirements may apply when a Remote Online Notary platform or another third-party service is used.

02

Business Owner

RCW Sr. Notary Services is owned and operated by Romando C. Williams Sr., a commissioned Florida Notary Public.

Information We Collect

Only what is reasonably needed.

Information may be collected when you submit an appointment request, contact the business, request pricing, or otherwise communicate with RCW Sr. Notary Services.

The specific information collected depends on the service you request.

Information You May Provide

Appointment and contact information.

01

Contact Information

Your name, email address, telephone number, and other contact information you choose to provide.

02

Location Information

City, state, ZIP code, appointment address, facility information, and other location details needed for a mobile appointment.

03

Scheduling Information

Preferred appointment date, time, alternate time, time zone, urgency, and related scheduling information.

04

Service Information

Whether you need Remote Online Notary or mobile service, general document type, number of signers, estimated number of notarial acts, and other service-related information.

05

Accessibility Information

Information you voluntarily provide about mobility, facility access, communication needs, transportation issues, or other circumstances that may affect the appointment.

06

Communications

Questions, messages, appointment details, and other information you send through the website, email, phone, or other business communication channels.

Do Not Submit Through the General Website Form

Keep sensitive information out of ordinary messages.

Financial Information

Do not submit bank account numbers, credit card numbers, PINs, passwords, or other financial credentials through the general appointment request form.

Government Identifiers

Do not submit Social Security numbers, complete driver's-license numbers, passport numbers, or similar sensitive identifiers unless specifically requested through an appropriate secure process.

Full Documents

Do not paste complete legal, medical, financial, or other sensitive documents into a general website message field.

How Information Is Used

Business purposes only.

Information collected through the website may be used to respond to inquiries, determine the requested service, provide estimates, calculate mobile travel charges, schedule appointments, communicate appointment requirements, and provide notary services.

Information may also be used for basic business administration, recordkeeping, fraud prevention, security, legal compliance, and resolving customer-service issues.

Third-Party Services

Some services require outside technology.

01

Remote Online Notary Platforms

Remote Online Notarization requires specialized technology.

A RON platform may collect identification information, identity-verification data, electronic signatures, documents, audio-video recordings, transaction records, and other information required to complete and retain the online notarization.

The platform's own privacy policy and terms govern its handling of that information.

02

Website and Business Providers

RCW Sr. Notary Services may use third-party providers for website hosting, forms, email, scheduling, payments, mapping, analytics, or other business functions.

Information may be transmitted to those providers when reasonably necessary to provide the requested service.

Information Sharing

Customer information is not sold.

RCW Sr. Notary Services does not sell customer personal information.

01

Service Providers

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website, process an appointment, provide Remote Online Notary services, process payments, calculate travel, communicate with customers, or perform other business functions.

02

Legal Requirements

Information may be disclosed when required by law, court order, legal process, regulatory obligation, lawful government request, or when reasonably necessary to protect the business, customers, or others from fraud, abuse, or security threats.

Data Security

Reasonable safeguards matter.

RCW Sr. Notary Services uses reasonable administrative and technical practices intended to protect business information.

No website, email system, internet connection, or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Customers should avoid sending highly sensitive information through ordinary email or general website forms unless specifically instructed to use a secure process.

Retention

Information is kept only as needed.

Appointment and business information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, bookkeeping, legal compliance, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and legitimate business records.

Remote Online Notary records may be subject to separate retention requirements under Florida law and the policies of the RON technology provider.

Website Data

Basic technical information may be collected.

01

Server and Technical Data

Website hosting providers may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, page requests, timestamps, and security logs.

02

Cookies and Analytics

The website may use necessary cookies or privacy-conscious analytics in the future.

This policy will be updated if additional tracking, advertising, or analytics technologies are added.

Your Choices

You control what you submit.

Correct Information

If you believe information you provided is inaccurate, contact RCW Sr. Notary Services and request a correction when appropriate.

Ask About Information

Limit What You Send
