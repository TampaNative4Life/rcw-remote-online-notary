Remote Online Notary

Notarize from your device.

Remote Online Notarization uses live audio-video communication and electronic documents rather than requiring the signer and notary to be in the same room.

Identity verification and other required procedures are handled through the approved RON process and technology platform.

Remote Online Notary Questions

Before your online appointment.

01

What is Remote Online Notarization?

Remote Online Notarization, commonly called RON, allows an eligible notarial act to be completed electronically while the signer and notary communicate through live audio and video.

02

What equipment do I need?

You generally need a compatible computer, tablet, or smartphone with reliable internet access, a working camera, a microphone, and the ability to complete the RON platform's identity-verification process.

03

Can I use my phone?

Possibly. Device compatibility depends on the RON platform and the identification process used for your session.

A computer or tablet may be easier for documents with multiple pages or signatures.

04

Can every document be notarized online?

No. Document eligibility and transaction requirements can vary.

I review the basic information before confirming a RON appointment.

05

How do you verify my identity online?

The RON platform uses the identity-verification procedures required for the transaction and applicable Florida requirements.

You receive instructions before the session.

06

Can you accommodate Pacific Time?

Yes. RCW Sr. Notary Services is designed to accommodate clients in other time zones.

Always provide your local time zone when requesting an appointment.

Mobile Notary

I can come to you.

Mobile service is available for clients who need or prefer an in-person appointment at their location.

That can include homes, offices, hospitals, rehabilitation facilities, assisted-living communities, nursing facilities, and other agreed locations.

Mobile Notary Questions

When you need me to come to you.

01

Where will you travel?

Mobile appointments are offered within the RCW Sr. service area and surrounding communities based on availability.

Longer trips may require a custom quote.

02

Where does the travel calculation start?

Mobile travel calculations use ZIP Code 33594 as the RCW Sr. service-area starting point.

03

How is my travel charge calculated?

The travel calculation uses round-trip driving distance, configured vehicle fuel economy, and the current RCW travel calculation rate.

Your estimated travel charge is provided before the appointment is confirmed.

04

Will you come to a hospital?

Yes, subject to availability, facility rules, access, parking, and the signer's ability to participate in the required notarial process.

05

Will you visit assisted living or a nursing facility?

Yes, subject to availability and facility access requirements.

Include the facility name, address, room information if applicable, and any entry instructions with your request.

06

Can someone else schedule for the signer?

Yes. A family member, caregiver, facility representative, or other person may request the appointment.

The actual signer must still satisfy the requirements for the requested notarial act.

Identification & Signing

Be ready before we start.

01

What identification do I need?

Identification requirements depend on the type of notarization and whether the appointment is in person or remote.

Have current government-issued identification available unless we have discussed another legally acceptable identification method.

02

Should I sign the document before the appointment?

Do not pre-sign a document when the required notarial act requires you to sign in the notary's presence.

If you are unsure, wait until the appointment.

03

Does the document need to be complete?

Yes. Bring or upload the complete document.

Do not intentionally remove pages that belong to the document.

04

Can you notarize for someone who cannot sign normally?

Some situations involving physical limitations may have legally permitted alternatives.

Describe the circumstances when requesting the appointment so the situation can be reviewed before travel or scheduling.

Signer Awareness

The signer must participate.

A notary must be able to complete the required notarial process with the signer.

A family member, caregiver, or facility representative cannot answer questions or make decisions on behalf of the signer when the law requires the signer's own participation.

If there are concerns about communication, awareness, willingness, or physical ability, tell me before the appointment.

Witnesses

Do not assume the notary is your witness.

01

Does my document need witnesses?

Some documents or transactions require witnesses and others do not.

The person or organization that prepared or will receive your document should tell you whether witnesses are required.

02

Do you provide witnesses?

Do not assume witnesses will automatically be available.

Tell me when requesting the appointment if your document requires witnesses so arrangements can be discussed.

Legal Questions

What a notary can and cannot do.

01

Can you tell me which notarial certificate I need?

No. A notary who is not acting as your attorney cannot choose the required notarial act for you.

Ask the document preparer, receiving organization, or an attorney if the document does not tell you what is required.

02

Can you explain what my document means?

I can explain the notarial process.

I cannot interpret your document, explain your legal rights, recommend legal language, or provide legal advice.

03

Can you prepare my legal document?

Not as part of my notary service.

Obtain the document from the appropriate source or seek legal assistance when document preparation or legal advice is needed.

04

Can a notarization be refused?

Yes.

A notarization may not proceed when legal requirements cannot be satisfied, identification is insufficient, the signer is unwilling or unable to participate as required, the requested act is prohibited, or other circumstances prevent a lawful notarization.
