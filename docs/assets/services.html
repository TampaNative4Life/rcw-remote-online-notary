Remote Online Notary

Notarize without driving anywhere.

Eligible documents can be notarized through a secure, Florida-compliant Remote Online Notary platform. You connect from a compatible device, complete the required identity verification, meet with me live online, and complete the notarization electronically.

01

Good Fit for Remote Notarization

  • Busy professionals
  • West Coast clients
  • Night-shift workers
  • Travelers
  • Parents with limited availability
  • People without convenient transportation
  • Clients who prefer to stay home
  • Last-minute eligible document needs

02

What You Will Need

  • A compatible computer, tablet, or smartphone
  • Reliable internet connection
  • Working camera and microphone
  • Acceptable government-issued identification
  • Your document in an acceptable electronic format
  • A quiet place for the live notarization session
  • Ability to complete the platform's identity-verification process

Remote Process

Simple steps.

First, send your appointment request and basic document information.

I review the request and confirm whether the service can be handled through Remote Online Notarization.

Once confirmed, you receive instructions for the secure online session. The RON platform handles the required identity-verification, electronic document process, signatures, and audio-video session.

Before Your Appointment

Have the basics ready.

01

Identification

Have acceptable identification available for each signer.

02

Complete Document

Bring or upload the complete document. Do not intentionally remove pages that belong to the document.

03

Do Not Pre-Sign

If the required notarial act requires signing in the notary's presence, wait until the appointment before signing.

04

Know What You Need

A notary cannot choose a notarial act for you or provide legal advice. If you are unsure what certificate is required, ask the document recipient, document preparer, or an attorney.
