Documents & Notarial Services

What it is. What it is used for. How I can help.

If your document already tells you which notarial act is required, I can perform that act after verifying the signer and completing the required notarial certificate.

I

Acknowledgments

What it is: You acknowledge that you signed a document willingly and for its stated purpose.

Common uses: Deeds, powers of attorney, contracts, property documents and other important agreements.

How I help: I verify your identity, receive your acknowledgment and complete the appropriate notarial certificate.

Request an Appointment
II

Jurats, Oaths & Affirmations

What it is: You swear or affirm that the statements in a document are true.

Common uses: Affidavits, sworn statements, applications and other documents requiring an oath or affirmation.

How I help: I verify your identity, administer the oath or affirmation, witness the signing when required and complete the certificate.

Request an Appointment
III

Affidavits & Sworn Statements

What it is: A written statement of facts that the signer swears or affirms is true.

Common uses: Insurance claims, residency or identity statements, business matters and other formal declarations.

How I help: When notarization is required, I administer the oath or affirmation and notarize the signer’s signature.

Request an Appointment
IV

Powers of Attorney

What it is: A legal document used to authorize another person to act on someone’s behalf.

Common uses: Financial, property, business and personal affairs.

How I help: I can perform the required notarial act on a properly prepared document. I do not choose, prepare or explain the legal powers granted by it.

Request an Appointment
V

Real Estate & Property Documents

What it is: Documents involving ownership, transfer or other matters concerning real property.

Common uses: Deeds, property transfers, affidavits and other property transactions.

How I help: I can notarize signatures when the document contains or is accompanied by the appropriate certificate. I do not provide title, real-estate or legal advice.

VI

Vehicle Titles & Bills of Sale

What it is: Documents used to record or support the sale, transfer or ownership of a vehicle.

Common uses: Private sales, title transfers and other vehicle transactions.

How I help: I can notarize signatures on properly prepared vehicle documents when a notarial act is required.

VII

VIN Verification

What it is: Verification of a vehicle identification number on appropriate Florida vehicle documentation.

Common uses: Certain title or registration transactions where the VIN must be physically verified.

How I help: I meet you and the vehicle, inspect the VIN and complete the notary portion of the appropriate verification form when permitted.

VIII

Medical & Health-Care Documents

What it is: Documents recording health-care instructions, authorizations or related personal decisions.

Common uses: Health-care directives, medical authorizations and other prepared documents requiring notarization.

How I help: I can travel to homes, hospitals, rehabilitation centers and care facilities. I notarize the signature, not the medical or legal choices in the document.

Business & Financial Documents

What it is: Agreements, authorizations, certifications and other business or financial documents.

Common uses: Contracts, corporate documents, financial authorizations and sworn business statements.

How I help: I verify the signer and perform the notarial act called for by the completed document.

X

School, Travel & Minor Consent

What it is: Written permissions or authorizations involving a child, student or traveler.

Common uses: Travel consent, school permissions and other situations where a notarized parent or guardian signature is requested.

How I help: I can notarize the signature on a properly prepared consent or authorization form.

XI

Certified Photocopies

What it is: A Florida notary may attest that a photocopy is a true copy of certain original documents.

Common uses: When an organization requests a notary-attested copy of an eligible original.

How I help: I can make or supervise the copy and complete the certificate when Florida law permits. Some public or vital records must instead come from the official records custodian.

XII

Safe-Deposit Box Contents

What it is: Florida law allows a notary to certify the contents of a safe-deposit box in circumstances authorized by law.

Common uses: A formal inventory or certification when a box is opened under an applicable legal procedure.

How I help: When the statutory requirements are met, I can perform the authorized certification associated with the box contents.

Wedding Officiant

Wedding ceremonies & vow renewals.

Florida notaries are authorized to solemnize marriages. For a legal marriage ceremony, the couple must have a valid marriage license before the ceremony.

How I help: I officiate the ceremony, complete the required certification on the marriage license and handle my officiant responsibilities.
